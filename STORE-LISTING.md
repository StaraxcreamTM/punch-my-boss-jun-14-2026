## Full description  (max 4000 chars)

Bad day at work? Clock in, glove up, and give your boss what the workday won't let you.

**Punch My Boss** is a one-thumb cartoon boxing game about the most satisfying overtime you'll ever pull. Tap to throw hands, read your boss's wind-up, slip the punch, and counter — then fill the K.O. meter and launch him clean out of the office.

It plays like a pocket Punch-Out with a comedy streak: every boss telegraphs before he swings. Watch the tell, swipe to dodge or duck, or time a risky parry to stagger him wide open and tee up a combo. Land clean hits to build Frenzy and Adrenaline, then unload.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• One-thumb controls — tap to punch (body or head), swipe to dodge left/right/duck, swipe up to parry. Built for playing one-handed on the commute.
• 20 bosses across a 4-act career — climb from New Hire to Boss of Bosses through the cubicle farm, the corner office, and worse. Each act, the fights get meaner.
• Themed showdowns — take the fight out of the office: construction sites, hospitals, gas stations, courtrooms and more, each with its own twist.
• Endless Survival — a back-to-back gauntlet of random bosses that get tougher every round. How long can you last?
• Daily Grievance — a fresh challenge every day, from Glass Jaw to Iron Boss to Sudden Death. Build a streak.
• Career ranks & grievance points — earn a promotion you actually want, from Intern all the way up.
• Your Record — total punches thrown, longest combo, and your boss's worst day, all tracked.
• Customise your boss — because it's more satisfying when it's personal.
• A Smart Boss that adapts — the harder you fight, the harder he fights back (toggle it off if you'd rather he didn't).

Quick to pick up, easy to rage-quit your actual stress into. No two-hour tutorial, no energy timers between you and a good punch — just the office, the boss, and your right hook.

Cartoon violence only. Nobody real gets hurt. Probably therapeutic. Definitely not legal advice.

Punch in. Punch up. Punch My Boss.

## Decisions to make (yours)

1. **Title** — `Punch My Boss` is clean and searchable; the subtitle variants
   add keywords ("Office Rage", "Stress Relief") if ASO wants them.
2. **Pricing line** — MAKE-IT-SELLABLE leans *free with a one-time unlock after
   ~3 bosses*. The copy above deliberately makes **no** price/ads claim so it
   fits either model. If you go paid-up-front or "no ads, pay once," add one
   honest line (e.g. "Pay once. No ads, no energy timers.") — don't claim it
   until it's true in the build.
3. **Content rating** — cartoon violence is a Teen-ish rating. The toilet-humour
   themed levels (if kept) will push it up and may cost some markets; decide
   deliberately at questionnaire time (see MAKE-IT-SELLABLE §7).
4. **Screenshots (5–6)** — each should show a *different* boss or gimmick: a KO
   mid-launch, a parry stagger, Endless, the Daily card, the career/acts screen,
   a themed level. The icon: the boss's face mid-punch.
5. **Trailer (15–30s)** — sells comedy games harder than screenshots; a capture
   of attract mode (v0.79) is a ready-made starting reel.
